Webcam & viewpoint

Set the measured straight-line distance from your eyes to the lens, hold still, then calibrate. The camera determines your eye direction and position. Dragging while your eyes are detected applies the new distance to the current camera observations.

The panda starts 8 cm in front of the seam; the room extends farther behind it. This depth separation makes the panda shift more strongly than the distant room as you move. Negative values move it behind the seam.

Tracking webcam placement

Default mount: centered above the shared top corner, facing into the room toward you. Positions are measured from the seam; height follows the screen’s top edge.

Match these values to the physical webcam mount. Recalibrate after changing its position, tilt, field of view, or screen dimensions.

Portrait screen setup

Drag left to reduce duplicated content at the corner; drag right to show more shared content on both screens. This shifts each view by the selected percentage of its panel width without resizing the panda. Zero restores the calibrated projection. Applies to separate display windows too.

Enter each panel’s visible dimensions. Span both inward-facing screens, or open a window for each. The border is one flat virtual opening projected onto the 90° panels. Its edges can look bent in a flat preview; judge alignment from your calibrated eye position on the physical screens. Press H to hide setup and the seam guide.
